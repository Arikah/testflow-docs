\# Troubleshooting \& FAQ



Frequently encountered issues and resolution steps.



\---



\### The application crashes immediately on launch



\* Verify that your operating system meets the minimum requirements.

\* On Windows, try launching the application by right-clicking the icon and selecting \*\*Run as administrator\*\*.

\* Check your antivirus or firewall settings to ensure the application binary is not quarantined.



\---



\### Data does not load or views appear empty



\* Check active filters at the top of the list view to confirm items are not simply hidden.

\* Verify network connectivity if your workspace connects to a remote server or database.

\* Sign out and sign back in to refresh cached session tokens.
