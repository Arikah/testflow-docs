# Quick Setup

Configure your workspace in a few simple steps after the first launch.

---

## Sign In

1. Launch the application.
2. Enter your account credentials or create a new account.
3. Complete the initial license verification. An active internet connection is recommended during this step.

---

## Choose Your Workspace

1. Create a new workspace or open an existing one.
2. Set your preferred language and time zone.
3. Customize the default dashboard layout to match your workflow.

---

## Sync Options

Enable cloud auto-sync to keep your revisions backed up across devices. If you prefer to work offline, remember to save manual revisions before closing the application.
